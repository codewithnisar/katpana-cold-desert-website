01 — THE NATURAL WONDER

AN ALPINE DESERT.
LIKE NO OTHER ON EARTH.

Katpana Desert, also known as the Cold Desert of Skardu, is recognized as one of the world's highest altitude cold deserts.

Unlike typical warm low-elevation deserts, Katpana sits surrounded by the soaring peaks of the Karakoram and Himalayan mountain ranges. Here, wind-sculpted sand dunes shift against a dramatic backdrop of icy summits and clear azure skies.

04 — SEASONAL GUIDE

KATPANA,
THROUGH THE SEASONS.

Every season brings a fresh perspective to Katpana's high-altitude climate.

SUMMER
01 / MAY – AUG

Warm Dunes & Blue Skies

Temperatures range from 15°C to 28°C. Ideal season for 4x4 dune safari, camping, and clear mountain vistas.

Temp: 15°C - 28°C Access: Open
AUTUMN
02 / SEP – NOV

Golden Poplars & Crisp Air

Cool crisp weather (5°C to 18°C) with vibrant golden leaves across the Skardu valley against white dunes.

Temp: 5°C - 18°C Access: Open
WINTER
03 / DEC – FEB

Combine your visit to Katpana Cold Desert with these legendary nearby destinations in Gilgit-Baltistan.

Shangrila & Kachura Lakes

Emerald alpine lakes surrounded by pine forests, located just 25 minutes from Katpana.

The 'Land of Giants' — world's second-highest plateau, home to Himalayan brown bears and wildflowers.

16th-century King of Forts standing high on a cliff offering panoramic views of Skardu Valley and Indus River.

400-year-old restored Raja palace in the gateway to K2 and Karakoram mountain expeditions.

08 — VISITOR FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Katpana Desert is located just 15 minutes drive (9 km) from Skardu main bazaar and 20 minutes from Skardu Airport (FKU). Taxis, private cars, and rental 4x4 jeeps are readily available across Skardu town.

The main entrance roads leading to Katpana village are paved and accessible by normal cars. However, driving deep into the soft sand dunes requires a 4x4 vehicle or quad bike. Local drivers offer dune safari rides at the entrance.

Yes! Katpana offers luxury insulated stargazing glamping resorts as well as traditional camping sites. Sleeping under the crisp, clear Karakoram night sky is one of the top recommended experiences in Skardu.

Public entry to view and walk across Katpana Cold Desert dunes is free. Optional paid activities include 4x4 dune rides, quad biking, camel rides, and glamping stays.

Yes, Katpana Desert is open year-round! In winter (December to February), visitors witness white snow covering the warm golden sand dunes against snow-capped peaks. Heavy warm winter attire is recommended.
